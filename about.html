About Us

We Deliver The Quality Water.

Acuas is a leading provider of pure, healthy drinking water. With over 20 years of experience in the water industry, we are committed to delivering the highest quality water to homes and businesses across the region. Our journey began in 2004 with a simple mission: to make clean, safe drinking water accessible to everyone.

Our state-of-the-art purification process removes all impurities while retaining essential minerals that are good for your health. We believe that access to clean water is a basic right, and we are dedicated to making it available to every household and business. From our purification plant to your doorstep, we maintain the highest quality standards.

Today, Acuas serves thousands of satisfied customers with our reliable water delivery service. We take pride in our commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction. Whether you need water for your home, office, or business, we have the perfect solution for you.

100% Pure Water

Our advanced multi-stage filtration system ensures every drop is 100% pure, safe, and free from harmful contaminants. We test every batch in our certified laboratory.

Reliable Delivery Service

We deliver fresh, pure water directly to your doorstep within 24 hours of order placement. Our fleet of delivery vehicles ensures timely and safe delivery.

Our Mission

Our mission at Acuas is to provide every household and business with access to pure, safe, and affordable drinking water. We strive to promote health and wellness through quality hydration, support sustainable water practices, and build lasting relationships with our customers and communities.

We work tirelessly to ensure that every drop of water we deliver meets the highest standards of purity and quality. Our commitment to excellence drives us to continuously improve our services and expand our reach.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the most trusted water delivery brand in the world, known for our unwavering commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction. We envision a future where everyone has access to clean drinking water, and where water conservation and sustainability are prioritized.

We dream of a world where no one has to worry about the safety of their drinking water. At Acuas, we are working every day to make this vision a reality by delivering pure water and promoting water awareness.

What makes Acuas water different?

Acuas water goes through a multi-stage purification process that removes 99.9% of impurities while retaining essential minerals that are good for your health. We test every batch for quality in our certified laboratory.

How often do you deliver water?

We offer flexible delivery schedules based on your needs. You can choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly deliveries. We also offer emergency delivery services for urgent needs.

Is your water safe for everyone?

Yes! Our water is 100% safe for all ages. It is tested regularly in our certified laboratory to ensure it meets and exceeds all safety standards.

Do you offer bulk discounts for businesses?

Yes, we offer competitive bulk pricing for businesses, offices, and large families. Contact us for a customized quote based on your needs and consumption.
